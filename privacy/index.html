Focus Ring — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Focus Ring (“the App”) is a kitchen countdown timer. This policy describes how the App handles data on your phone. It is a separate product from MGH Pulse and does not use hospital accounts or roster data.

On-device only

Timer state, history, Focus Garden rewards, and settings stay on your device. The release App does not create an account, does not show ads, and does not collect or share personal data. The Android release does not use the internet. On iPhone, StoreKit checks the App Store for a verified Pro purchase; Focus Ring does not receive your Apple Account credentials.

Data we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs that profile you.
  • No location, contacts, photos, messages, or identifiers sent off the device.

What stays on the phone

  • Timer state — remaining time, phase, and loop settings so a session can continue after you leave the App.
  • History and Focus Garden — completed sessions you choose to keep locally, shown as earned leaves in Focus Tree or petals in Bloom Ring.
  • Preferences — sound, volume, overlay size, and similar options.

You can delete session history and Focus Garden rewards in the App. That does not stop an active timer, reset preferences, or remove a verified Pro purchase. Removing the App asks your device to remove local data. Your operating system may keep or restore app data through its own backup settings; Focus Ring does not operate a backup server.

Notifications and alarms

Notifications and alarms are scheduled on the phone so a countdown can finish while you use other apps. On supported iPhones, Live Activity shows the local countdown. On Android, optional overlay permission keeps the timer visible over other apps. These features do not send your session data to a server.

Children

The App is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes

If how Focus Ring handles data changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date.
