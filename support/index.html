Frequently asked questions

How do I restore Focus Ring Pro?

Open Focus Ring, go to Settings → Unlock Pro, and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought Pro with. Focus Ring has no separate account. A verified entitlement is required before extra alarms and history unlock.

What does Pro include?

A one-time purchase that adds extra alarm sounds and on-device session history. The kitchen timer itself stays free.

The alarm didn't ring.

  1. Open Focus Ring and confirm notifications are enabled in Settings.
  2. On iPhone, allow Time Sensitive Notifications when prompted.
  3. Keep system date and time set automatically.
  4. Restart the current phase if the countdown was changed outside the app.
  5. On Android, open Settings → Reliability and allow unrestricted battery for Focus Ring.

Live Activity is missing.

Live Activity is free on supported iPhones. If it is not visible, check Settings → Focus Ring → Live Activities, then start a new timer phase. On iPad, Focus Ring runs as an iPhone app and does not show a Live Activity.

The purchase didn't complete.

Check your internet connection and that you're signed in to the App Store, then try again. If the product does not load, the app retries once and then shows “Store unavailable.” If you were charged but Pro is still locked, tap Restore Purchase.

Is my data collected?

No. Timer state, settings, and history remain on your device. Focus Ring has no account, ads, or analytics. Read the Focus Ring Privacy Policy.
